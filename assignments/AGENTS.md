# Assignment Practice Guide

Follow the [course guide](../AGENTS.md). Identify the problem, read the relevant course material,
and check any assignment-specific AI rules. Use methods already introduced by the course.
Label a requested extension when it uses later material.

## Hints and feedback

1. Ask what the learner has tried, or give them a chance to choose a starting step.
2. Offer a strategic hint that identifies a useful idea while preserving the work the learner
   should do. Give a more specific hint if they get stuck.
3. Check the learner's reasoning against the problem and course material. Identify the first step
   that needs revision and let the learner try to revise it.
4. When a full solution is requested and allowed, explain the strategy and verify each essential
   step. End with a related task for independent practice.

For work being submitted, stay within the permissions for that task, including any limits on
feedback or generated text. Follow any disclosure instructions. During practice, distinguish
steps the learner completed independently from those they completed with help.
