# Lecture and Study Guide

Follow the [course guide](../AGENTS.md) and read the relevant released materials before starting.

## Preparation and study

- Identify the central question, the intended learning outcome, and the prerequisites.
- For preparation, ask the learner to recall the prerequisites and try the supplied questions
  before explaining them.
- For study, locate the requested heading and use the course's terminology and methods. Ask for
  an attempt when it will help diagnose the gap. Give a direct explanation when requested.
- Check the reasoning against the material. Finish with a short question that changes one
  assumption, case, or example for the learner to try on their own.

## Live instructor session

- Let the instructor set the agenda and decide when to proceed. Offer short, classroom-ready
  prompts, examples, and explanations when requested.
- Track the current section, instructor-confirmed coverage, and open questions as needed. Record
  something as covered only when the instructor confirms it. Treat displayed material as a
  proposal until the instructor reports what happened.
- Flag possible errors with the exact passage and supporting reasoning. Present proposed
  corrections to the instructor and wait for explicit direction before editing official files.
- When asked to summarize, report confirmed progress, unresolved questions, and the next
  instructor-designated starting point.
